# Jawab Data Processing Addendum

This template supports early client conversations. It is not legal advice.

## Purpose

Jawab processes limited patient communication data only to qualify, route, recover, and report patient inquiries for the clinic.

## Data Processed

Jawab may process:

- patient name.
- phone or WhatsApp number.
- language preference.
- service interest.
- urgency.
- preferred callback time.
- conversation summary.
- source channel.
- next action.

Jawab should avoid storing unnecessary medical details.

## Data Ownership

The clinic owns the patient relationship and patient data. Jawab acts as an operational service provider.

## Access

Access is limited to:

- Jawab operator.
- authorized clinic staff.
- approved delivery tools needed for the workflow.

## Third-Party Tools

Possible tools include Google Sheets, Gmail, Make, Twilio WhatsApp, Cal.com, Loom, Vercel, Vapi, Retell, or Ultravox, depending on the active workflow.

Only tools needed for the agreed service should be used.

## Retention

Operational logs should be deleted or exported within 30 days of contract termination unless the clinic requests a different retention period.

## Security

Jawab will use reasonable access controls, avoid public patient-data sharing, and avoid exposing patient details in demos, public content, or case studies.

## Review And Case Study Data

Case studies must use anonymized data unless the clinic gives written approval to use its name.

## Clinic Responsibility

The clinic remains responsible for legal compliance in its jurisdiction, clinical decision-making, and patient care.
