# Jawab Privacy Trust Block

Use this text in proposals, landing pages, and when a clinic asks about data.

## Front-Facing Trust Block

### English

**Privacy-first by design.**

Jawab does not provide medical advice, diagnose patients, or replace clinical judgment. We only handle operational routing data: name, contact, language, service interest, urgency, preferred callback time, source, and next action.

The clinic owns the patient relationship and data. Demos and case studies never expose patient details without written approval.

We do not broadcast, spam, or cold-message patients. Jawab only handles recovery and follow-up paths the clinic approves, with opt-out rules and limited operational data.

### Arabic

**الخصوصية أولاً.**

جواب لا يقدم استشارات طبية ولا يحل محل القرارات السريرية. نتعامل فقط مع بيانات التوجيه التشغيلية: الاسم، طريقة التواصل، اللغة، الخدمة المطلوبة، مدى الاستعجال، وقت الاتصال المفضل، المصدر، والخطوة التالية.

العيادة تملك علاقة المريض وبياناته. العروض التوضيحية ودراسات الحالة لا تكشف أي تفاصيل للمرضى بدون موافقة خطية.

## Data We Touch

- Patient name
- Phone / WhatsApp number
- Language preference
- Service interest
- Urgency level
- Preferred callback time
- Source (missed call / WhatsApp / IG / website)
- Next action
- Booking status
- Staff handoff status

## Data We Never Touch

- Medical records
- Clinical diagnoses
- Treatment plans or clinical notes
- Insurance details (unless clinic explicitly asks us to mention coverage)
- Patient photos or clinical images
- Payment or financial information

## Objection Responses

### "We are concerned about patient data."

```
Fair concern. Jawab only uses the minimum information needed to qualify and route the patient: name, contact, service interest, urgency, and next action. The clinic owns the patient relationship and data. We avoid medical advice, avoid unnecessary clinical detail, and keep logs limited to operational recovery. We have a formal Data Processing Addendum if your compliance team needs it.
```

### "Will this spam patients?"

```
No. Jawab is not a broadcast tool. It only handles recovery and follow-up paths the clinic approves, with opt-out rules and limited operational data. Every message template is approved by the clinic before it goes live.
```

### "Is our data stored securely?"

```
We use Google Workspace (Sheets, Drive) and industry-standard tools. Patient data is limited to operational routing info and is never shared with third parties. The clinic can request data deletion at any time.
```
