# Jawab Service Agreement

This template is for the first founding clients. It is not legal advice.

## Parties

Provider: `[Your Name]`, operating as `Jawab`.

Client: `[Clinic Legal Name]`.

Effective date: `[Date]`.

## Services

Jawab will provide the selected service package:

- `Jawab Audit`: clinic revenue leak audit.
- `Jawab Recover`: missed inquiry recovery, Arabic/English qualification, booking link or staff handoff, Treatment Plan Rescue, AI Staff Handoff Summary, and weekly Jawab Intelligence Report.
- Optional add-ons only if agreed in writing: `Jawab Voice`, `Jawab Brain`, `Jawab Web`, `Jawab Acquire`, reviews, staff training, or custom reporting.

## Exclusions

Unless separately agreed, Jawab does not provide:

- PMS/EHR integration.
- medical advice.
- guaranteed revenue or guaranteed booked appointments.
- 24/7 human support.
- full receptionist replacement.
- paid ads management.
- voice routing through clinic phone systems.

## Fees

`Jawab Recover`: AED `5,000` setup including the first 30 days, then AED `1,500/month`.

Founding client floor, if approved: AED `3,500` upfront.

Monthly fees are due on the first day of each monthly service period.

Setup begins after payment clears or proof of transfer is received.

## Client Responsibilities

Client will provide:

- clinic hours.
- service list.
- booking link or booking process.
- escalation contact.
- approved WhatsApp greeting.
- review link if review automation is used.
- access or information needed to configure agreed workflows.

Client remains responsible for clinical decisions, pricing decisions, patient care, and final booking confirmation.

## Data Handling

Jawab will only use the minimum patient data required to qualify and route inquiries. Typical data includes name, contact, service interest, urgency, preferred callback time, source, and next action.

The clinic owns the patient relationship and patient data.

## Cancellation

Either party may cancel monthly service with 30 days written notice.

Setup fees are non-refundable after setup work begins.

## Limitation Of Liability

Jawab's liability is capped at one month of paid retainer fees. Jawab is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or lost-profit damages.
