# Jawab Objection Handling

Keep answers short. Redirect to the leak, the demo, or the audit.

## We Already Have A Receptionist

`Jawab does not replace reception. It catches the overflow: missed calls, delayed WhatsApp replies, after-hours inquiries, and patients who need follow-up after a consult. Your staff wakes up to a short list of qualified patients instead of chasing cold threads.`

## We Use Another Software

`Most clinic software handles scheduling after the patient reaches your process. Jawab handles the patients who never make it that far: missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies, consult drop-offs, and unanswered follow-ups.`

## How Do We Know It Works?

`The fastest answer is the demo and the audit. I can show you the recovery flow in 60 seconds, then run a leak audit on your clinic so we are talking about your actual missed paths, not theory.`

## It Is Too Expensive

`One recovered implant, veneer, or smile-design patient can cover Jawab for months. I do not claim guaranteed revenue, but the math is simple: if Jawab catches even one patient you would have lost, the system becomes very hard to justify not having.`

## We Need To Think About It

`That makes sense. While you think, I will send the leak audit so you have something concrete: missed call path, WhatsApp path, website friction, and where I think patients are dropping off.`

## We Are Concerned About Patient Data

`Fair concern. Jawab only uses the minimum information needed to qualify and route the patient: name, contact, service interest, urgency, and next action. The clinic owns the patient relationship and data. We avoid medical advice, avoid unnecessary clinical detail, and keep logs limited to operational recovery.`

## Can It Integrate With Our Clinic Software?

`Not in the first version, intentionally. PMS integrations add months of complexity and cost. Jawab works alongside your current workflow: staff gets a clear WhatsApp or Sheet summary and books the patient in the system they already use.`

## Are You Based In UAE?

`I am based in Egypt and building Jawab for Arabic-English clinics across the Gulf. Delivery is remote, but the clinic deployment runs through your approved channels, WhatsApp setup, and staff handoff process. For founding clinics, I personally handle setup and reporting.`

## Are You A Company?

`Jawab is currently founder-led. You receive a formal service agreement, invoice, data processing terms, and receipt. If your finance process requires a registered vendor, we can start with the audit first and work from there.`

## Do You Have Case Studies?

`Jawab is currently taking its first founding clinics. That is why the first implementation is hands-on and offered at the founding rate. I am not asking you to trust a vague promise. I will show the demo, run the leak audit on your clinic, and define exactly what we will install before you pay.`

## Can This Handle Arabic Properly?

`Yes. The workflow is Arabic-English by design. For patient-facing messages, we use approved clinic wording, and nothing goes live without your approval.`

## Will This Spam Patients?

`No. Jawab is not a broadcast tool. It only handles recovery and follow-up paths the clinic approves, with opt-out rules and limited operational data.`
