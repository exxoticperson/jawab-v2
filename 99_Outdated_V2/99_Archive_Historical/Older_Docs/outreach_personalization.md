# Skill: Outreach Personalization

## Goal

Convert a clinic row into a short, personalized first message that sounds credible and easy to reply to.

## Required Inputs

- clinic name
- doctor name if known
- pain proof
- best first channel
- offer angle

## Required Outputs

- first message
- short follow-up
- channel note

## Rules

- do not lead with AI
- keep the CTA to a `60-second demo`
- do not overload the opener with technical detail
- anchor on the pain proof immediately
