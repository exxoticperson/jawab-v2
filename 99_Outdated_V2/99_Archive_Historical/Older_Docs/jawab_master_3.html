The System Prompt — Your Core IP

This is what you must write and protect. A strong system prompt for a dental AI receptionist covers:

  • Clinic name, address, working hours, services offered, prices (if shared)
  • Greeting in Arabic with proper Islamic honorifics (السلام عليكم) + English fallback
  • Qualification flow: What's the reason for your visit → Is this urgent → Have you been here before → Preferred doctor (if applicable)
  • Booking trigger: When patient confirms → call Cal.com API to check availability → offer two slots → confirm booking → send WhatsApp confirmation
  • FAQ responses: Insurance, parking, what to bring, cancellation policy
  • Emergency escalation: If patient describes severe pain, swelling, bleeding → trigger live transfer or on-call SMS
  • Cultural layer: Ramadan timing awareness, patient privacy, gender-appropriate titles

08 / Legal & Contracts

Protect Yourself From Day One

Minimum Contract Terms to Include

  • Scope of service: Exactly what the AI handles and what it doesn't (does not replace the dentist, does not provide medical advice)
  • Payment terms: 100% of setup fee upfront before work begins. Monthly retainer charged on the 1st of each month.
  • Cancellation policy: 30-day written notice required. No refund of setup fee under any circumstances.
  • Data handling: Call recordings stored for 30 days. You are the processor, they are the controller. Simple GDPR/PDPL language.
  • Liability cap: Your liability is capped at the monthly retainer amount. You are not liable for missed bookings due to factors outside your control.
  • IP ownership: The system prompt and templates remain your IP. The clinic owns their own patient data.
  • Auto-renewal: Contract auto-renews monthly unless 30-day notice given.
Critical
Get a simple service agreement signed before you touch anything. Use a tool like PandaDoc (free tier) or DocuSign for signatures. A WhatsApp "yes" is not a contract. In the Gulf, written agreements are expected and respected.

Data Privacy — What You Must Know

  • UAE: The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) applies. You need a basic data processing agreement with the clinic. Retell AI is HIPAA-ready, which satisfies most healthcare privacy requirements.
  • KSA: PDPL 2023 — similar requirements. Patient data must be processed with consent.
  • Egypt: Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020. Less strictly enforced but growing. Still worth basic data agreement.
  • Your mitigation: Store no patient data on your own servers. All recordings and data live in Retell's infrastructure. You just access the logs.
