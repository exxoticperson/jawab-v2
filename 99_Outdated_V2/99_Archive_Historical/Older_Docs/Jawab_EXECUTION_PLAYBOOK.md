# Jawab Execution Playbook

## The Offer

Jawab is `Dental Patient Recovery Infrastructure`.

Lead with:

`Jawab helps private dental clinics recover missed, delayed, and post-consult patient conversations in Arabic and English before they leak to another clinic.`

Do not lead with:

- AI receptionist
- replace your front desk
- fully automated bookings

## Offer Ladder

1. `Jawab Recovery Sprint`
   - missed-call recovery
   - missed-inquiry recovery
   - bilingual qualification
   - booking link or handoff
   - weekly recovery report
2. `Jawab Consult Follow-up`
   - follow-up after implant, veneer, or cosmetic consults
3. `Jawab Reviews`
   - honest review request automation
4. `Jawab Voice`
   - only when routing economics make sense
5. `Jawab Digital Foundation`
   - bilingual website rebuild tied to recovery flows

## Pricing

- UAE founding offer: `AED 5,000 upfront` including setup + first 30 days, then `AED 1,500/month`
- UAE floor: `AED 3,500 upfront`
- Egypt semi-warm offer: `EGP 25,000 upfront`, then `EGP 7,500/month`
- Consult follow-up add-on: `AED 750-1,500/month`
- Reviews add-on: `AED 750-1,250/month`
- Website upsell: `AED 6,000-15,000`

## 3-Hour Daily Calendar

### Hour 1: Lead Gathering and Pain Discovery

- pull 10 clinics from the priority list
- verify Instagram, website, WhatsApp, and service focus
- call during lunch or a busy hour
- record one concrete pain proof

### Hour 2: Outreach Execution

- send personalized WhatsApp voice notes
- send IG DMs
- send manual emails
- ask 2 classmates, professors, or family contacts for intros

### Hour 3: Follow-up, Demos, Calls

- reply to every warm response
- send demo link immediately
- run discovery calls
- quantify missed revenue
- close on the Recovery Sprint

## Daily KPI Floor

- 10 newly qualified clinics researched
- 10 high-quality touches sent
- all warm replies answered same day

## Weekly KPI Floor

- 50 newly qualified clinics
- 50-70 high-quality touches
- 5+ live sales conversations

## First Outreach Formula

`I noticed [specific pain proof]. I built a bilingual recovery loop that catches these patients immediately. Want a 60-second demo?`

Pain-proof examples:

- no WhatsApp path
- missed lunch-hour call
- Google reviews mentioning booking friction
- broken website
- implant/cosmetic focus with slow response path

## Warm Network Opener

`I built an Arabic/English patient recovery system for missed calls and consult drop-off. Can I install the beta here this week and show you exactly what it catches?`

## Decision Rules

- voice is a demo weapon first
- WhatsApp recovery is the default deployment
- consult follow-up is the first expansion corridor
- reviews come next
- websites only after trust or when the site is obviously dead
- lead gen is a salvage branch, not the main business this month
