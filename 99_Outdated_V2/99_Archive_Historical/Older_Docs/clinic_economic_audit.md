# Skill: Clinic Economic Audit

## Goal

Turn a clinic's operational leak into a simple money argument without making fake guarantees.

## Use When

- preparing sales-call bullets
- writing pitch lines
- handling "why do we need this?"
- deciding which clinics deserve priority

## Inputs

- specialty focus
- likely treatment value
- pain proof
- reply speed or booking friction
- market context if available

## Calculation Style

Use ranges and conservative assumptions.

Examples:

- one missed implant consult can be worth thousands of AED
- a delayed WhatsApp reply can send a cosmetic patient to the next clinic
- post-consult follow-up matters because high-ticket patients often pause before paying

## Do Not

- promise exact recovered revenue
- invent missed-call volume
- use inflated math in outreach
- make medical claims

## Output

- conservative loss angle
- one call question
- one close-friendly ROI line
