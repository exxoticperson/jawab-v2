# Jawab Outreach Templates & Guidelines (V2 - High Conversion)

This document contains the approved messaging templates, objection handling scripts, and follow-up sequences for Jawab outreach. **All scripts are designed to sound like a human, not a bot.**

## The Golden Rules of Jawab Outreach
1. **Never send a wall of text.** People scan, they don't read.
2. **Lead with the pain.** Not with "AI" or "software".
3. **Be informal but respectful.** Use emojis sparingly.
4. **Don't ask them to buy.** Ask if they want to see a 60-second video. Low friction.

## Call Close Script (The 10-Minute Close)

**The goal is not to sound like a salesman. Sound like a peer.**

- **0–2 min (The Hook):** "I study dentistry, so I see this from the inside. Clinics lose patients every single day because of a missed call during lunch or a busy shift. Most don't even know how much they're losing."
- **2–4 min (Discovery):** "How does your front desk handle missed calls right now? Is there an automated system, or do they just try to call back when they have time?"
- **4–6 min (The Demo):** "Let me show you how Jawab works. Takes 60 seconds." *(Share screen, show demo).*
- **6–8 min (The ROI Reveal):** "Based on your size, if Jawab catches just 5 calls a week, that's 20 a month. Even if only 2 book a treatment, what's your average case value? AED 5,000? That's AED 10,000 recovered."
- **8–10 min (The Close):** "Setup is AED 5,000, which includes the first month. Then it's AED 1,500/month. For founding clinics this month, I'm doing the setup for AED 3,500. It comes with a 14-day guarantee: if we don't catch 5 patients, you don't pay. Should we get it installed?"
