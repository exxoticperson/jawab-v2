# Jawab — High-Value Individual Dentist Leads (Dubai & UAE)

> These are individual doctors (not just clinic pages) who are highly active on Instagram, care about their personal brand, and likely have their own patient influx that they need to manage. They are perfect targets for the "Empty Chair" or "Built by a Dentist" outreach angles.

## The Strategy for Pitching Individual Doctors
When DMing a doctor directly on their personal page:
1. **Never message them while they are working.** Message between 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM GST.
2. **Interact first.** Like 2 of their recent clinical posts, reply to a story if they have one ("Great margin on that crown, Doc"), THEN send the DM.
3. **The hook:** "Dr. [Name], your clinical work is incredible. Do you manage your own patient bookings from this page, or does the clinic handle it? Because you're probably losing 5-10 DMs/calls a week while you're chairside."

## How to Find More (The Infinite Lead Loop)
Instead of scraping, use this manual 5-minute hack on Instagram to find hundreds of doctors:

1. Go to any of the doctors above.
2. Click on the **small downward arrow** next to the "Message" / "Follow" button.
3. Instagram will open the **"Suggested for You"** carousel. 
4. This carousel will be 100% filled with other Dubai dentists. 
5. Scroll, click, and DM.
