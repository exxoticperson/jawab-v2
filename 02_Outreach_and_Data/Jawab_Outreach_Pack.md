Use one of these in the opener:

- `no WhatsApp path`
- `missed lunch-hour call`
- `reviews mentioning booking friction`
- `broken website`
- `implant/cosmetic focus with slow response path`

## Priority Language

Keep the language tight:

- `missed inquiries`
- `booking leakage`
- `post-consult follow-up`
- `Arabic and English`
- `booking link or staff handoff`
- `60-second demo`

## Do Not Say

- AI receptionist
- replace your front desk
- fully automated bookings
- lead generation as the opening pitch
- PMS integration as a day-one promise

## Reply Handling

If they answer:

1. send the demo
2. ask one discovery question
3. quantify the leak
4. close on Recovery Sprint

Discovery question:

```text
Roughly how are you handling missed calls and slow replies today?
```

If they ask price:

```text
The first sprint is AED 5,000 upfront, including setup and the first 30 days.
```

If they ask what comes after:

```text
After the recovery sprint, we can add consult follow-up, reviews, or voice where it makes sense.
```

## Follow-Up Rhythm

- Day 0: initial message
- Day 2: first follow-up
- Day 5: final follow-up
- stop after Day 5 if no reply
