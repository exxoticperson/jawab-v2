# Jawab Partnership Outreach

## Targets

- dental supply reps
- lab reps
- equipment reps
- professors
- classmates
- classmates with family clinics
- existing clinic owners after they are happy

## Supplier / Rep Ask

```text
I am building Jawab, a patient recovery system for dental clinics.

You already know clinics dealing with missed calls and slow follow-up. If you introduce me to a clinic that becomes a client, I will pay AED 500 or 10% of the setup fee.

Want me to send you a 60-second demo you can forward?
```

## Professor / Classmate Ask

```text
I am building Jawab, an Arabic-English patient recovery system for dental clinics.

I am looking for 2 founding clinics where I can run a free leak audit and show how missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies, and consult follow-up are handled.

Do you know a clinic owner or professor who would let me show it?
```

## Clinic Referral Ask

Use after the clinic has seen value.

```text
If Jawab has been useful, I would appreciate one intro to another clinic owner who deals with missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies, or consult drop-off.

If they become a client, I will add one free month to your Jawab account.
```

## Commission

- AED `500` per converted intro, or
- `10-15%` of the first setup fee

## Founding Client Letter Referral Line

`As a founding clinic, you are receiving the early Jawab rate for 12 months. In return, I would appreciate honest feedback, permission to use anonymized results in a case study, and one referral to another clinic if Jawab creates value for you.`
