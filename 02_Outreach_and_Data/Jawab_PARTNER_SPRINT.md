# Jawab Partner Sprint

Referral partners are a primary channel, not a side idea. Gulf clinics are relationship-driven.

## Why Partners Before Cold Outreach

A warm intro from a trusted supplier beats 50 cold DMs. Dental supply reps, lab techs, implant reps, and dental school contacts already sit inside clinic decision-making circles.

## Target Partners

### Tier 1 — High Trust (send first)

| Partner Type | Why They Work | How Many To Reach |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Dental implant reps | They visit premium clinics weekly. They know who's busy and who's losing leads. | 5–10 |
| Aligner reps (Invisalign, ClearCorrect) | They work with clinics doing high-ticket ortho. | 3–5 |
| Dental lab owners | They process cases and know which clinics are growing vs stalling. | 3–5 |
| Classmates with family clinics | Instant trust. Direct intro to owner/parent. | 5–10 |
| Professors with Gulf connections | Especially those who consult or own clinics. | 3–5 |

### Tier 2 — Useful (send second week)

| Partner Type | Why They Work | How Many To Reach |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Dental equipment reps | They sell chairs, units, imaging — they know who's investing. | 5 |
| Dental photographers/content creators | They work with premium clinics that care about image. | 3–5 |
| Clinic website/marketing agencies | They already have clinic trust but may not offer recovery. | 5 |
| Dental conference contacts | Met at events, shared a booth, connected on IG. | 5 |

## Commission Structure

| Outcome | Partner Earns |
|---------|---------------|
| Intro → clinic becomes paying Recover client | AED 500 or 10% of setup fee (whichever is higher) |
| Intro → clinic buys Visible Audit | AED 150 |
| Intro → clinic buys Recover + Visible bundle | AED 1,000 or 10% of setup fee |

Paid after client payment clears. No upfront cost to partners.

## Partner Outreach Message

### WhatsApp / DM

```
Hey [Name] — quick one.

I'm launching Jawab, a patient recovery system for premium dental clinics. It catches missed calls, delayed WhatsApp replies, and post-consult drop-offs.

If you know one clinic owner who complains about slow follow-up or missed inquiries, I'll pay AED 500 (or 10% of the setup fee) if they become a client.

Want me to send you the 60-second demo you can forward?
```

### For classmates specifically

```
Hey [Name] — I'm building something for dental clinics and I think [family clinic / your clinic] might find it useful.

It's called Jawab — it catches missed patient calls and WhatsApp inquiries and routes them back to staff before the patient goes somewhere else.

Would you or [parent/owner] be open to a 5-minute look at the demo? No pressure, just want honest feedback.
```

### For agencies specifically

```
Hey [Name] — I see you build websites/do marketing for clinics.

I'm building Jawab, a patient recovery layer that sits behind clinic websites: catches missed calls, slow WhatsApp replies, and post-consult drop-offs, then routes them back to staff.

Would you be interested in offering this to your clinic clients as an add-on? You introduce, I fulfill, you earn a referral fee per client.

Want me to send you the demo?
```

## Partner Follow-Up

### Day 4

```
Just checking if you had a chance to see the Jawab demo. If there is one clinic you think could use this, I can send you a 60-second audit I made for a similar clinic. Makes it easy to forward.
```

### Day 14

```
Quick update — I've been running leak audits on premium dental clinics in [city]. If any of your clinic contacts mention slow follow-up or missed patient calls, I'd appreciate the intro. The offer for you still stands.
```

## Tracking

Track partners in a simple Google Sheet:

```
Partner Name | Type | Date Contacted | Replied | Intro Made | Clinic Name | Outcome | Commission Paid
```

## Weekly Target

- Week 1: send to 20 partners
- Week 2: follow up all, send to 10 more
- Ongoing: 5 new partner contacts per week

## Referral From Existing Clients

After Day 30 with a paying client:

```
If Jawab has created value for [Clinic], I'd really appreciate one introduction to another clinic owner. In return, I'll extend your next month by one week free.
```

Keep this soft. Do not push referrals before value is proven.
