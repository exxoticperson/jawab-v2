# Skill: Offer Packaging

## Goal

Turn the Jawab offer into clean, close-ready assets without changing the strategy.

## Required Inputs

- current pricing
- current offer scope
- target market
- active proof points

## Required Outputs

- one-page offer copy
- short pitch language
- optional Loom or voiceover script

## Rules

- keep pricing consistent with the locked plan
- do not introduce older Jawab positioning
- keep the offer outcome-focused and easy to scan
