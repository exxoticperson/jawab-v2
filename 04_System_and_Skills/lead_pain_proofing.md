# Skill: Lead Pain Proofing

## Goal

Turn a clinic from partially researched into send-ready.

## Required Inputs

- clinic name
- specialty
- review count or reputation signal
- phone/mobile/WhatsApp path
- Instagram or website status

## Required Outputs

- `PainProof`
- `WhatsAppPath`
- `Channel`
- `NextAction`
- `OfferAngle`

## Rules

- prefer facts over assumptions
- use the shortest believable pain proof
- prioritize direct messaging paths
- tie the offer angle to lost patients, not generic automation
