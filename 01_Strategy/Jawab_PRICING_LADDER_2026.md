# Jawab Pricing Ladder 2026

## Founding Prices (Current)

### Free / Front-End

**Clinic Revenue Leak Mini-Audit**

- 5–10 minute personalized public-access test
- Shows 3–5 specific leaks
- Used as outreach weapon, not a product
- Goal: open the conversation

### Paid Entry

**Jawab Visible Audit — AED 1,500**

- Detailed AI/search/Google/WhatsApp visibility and leak audit
- For clinics not ready for Recover
- Credited toward Recover if they start within 7 days

### Core

**Jawab Recover — AED 5,000 setup including first 30 days, then AED 1,500/month**

Includes:

- Lead tracker (Google Sheets / Airtable)
- WhatsApp recovery scripts
- Missed call follow-up flow
- Instagram DM triage scripts
- Post-consult follow-up (Treatment Plan Rescue)
- AI Staff Handoff Summary
- Weekly Jawab Intelligence Report
- Staff handoff workflow

Founding floor: AED 3,500 upfront (first 1–2 clinics only)

### Premium

**Jawab Recover + Visible — AED 12,000 setup, AED 3,500/month**

Everything in Recover plus:

- Google/AI visibility audit
- Google Business Profile cleanup
- Review friction strategy
- Treatment-page recommendations
- AI search query tracking
- Weekly revenue + visibility intelligence report

### Flagship

**Jawab Clinic Revenue OS — AED 20,000 setup, AED 7,500/month**

Everything in Recover + Visible plus:

- Basic website/landing page improvements
- Owner dashboard
- Monthly strategy call
- Lead source intelligence
- Staff response scorecard

## Pricing Rules

- Do not quote AED to non-UAE clinics
- Round to clean numbers in local currency
- Never discount below founding floor
- Setup fee is always non-refundable after work begins
- Monthly fee is due on the first day of each service period
- Setup begins after payment clears or proof of transfer
- Visible Audit can be credited toward Recover within 7 days
