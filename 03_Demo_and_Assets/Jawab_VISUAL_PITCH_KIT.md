## CANVA STYLE GUIDE

**Prompt for Canva template search:**
"Professional dark pitch deck with gold accents"
or
"Minimal dark presentation template premium"

**Color Palette:**
- Background: #09090B (near-black)
- Text Primary: #FFFFFF
- Text Secondary: #86868B
- Accent Gold: #D4AF37
- WhatsApp Green: #25D366
- Alert Red: #FF453A
- Card Background: #111112
- Borders: rgba(255,255,255,0.08) → use #1A1A1A in Canva

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Inter Bold or Oswald Regular
- Body: Inter Regular
- Arabic: Tajawal Medium

**Design Rules:**
- Maximum 6 lines of text per slide
- Lots of whitespace
- Use the gold accent sparingly — only for key numbers and CTAs
- No stock photos of smiling doctors
- Use simple icons, not illustrations
- Every slide should feel like an Apple keynote
