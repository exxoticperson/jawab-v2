# Jawab Demo Polish Notes

Keep the current HTML demo. It is already strong enough to sell with.

## What Already Works

- cinematic lock-screen to missed-call transition
- WhatsApp-first recovery arc is easy to understand
- Arabic and English chat makes the product legible fast
- sound design and timing already make it feel premium

## Light Sharpening Only

1. Change the clinic framing from generic to clearly dental.
   - Replace `Smile Dental Clinic` with a more premium clinic label that matches the Jawab pitch.
   - Make the patient intent feel like a higher-value dental inquiry.

2. Tighten the first WhatsApp message.
   - Make it sound more like patient recovery and less like a generic support bot.
   - Keep it short, warm, and clinic-specific.

3. Strengthen the proof beat near the end.
   - Add one visible ROI cue such as `Recovered missed call`, `Qualified patient`, or `Booked consult`.
   - Keep it subtle, not dashboard-heavy.

4. Clarify what the clinic gets.
   - The alert scene should signal `staff handoff`, `booking secured`, or `patient recovered` more explicitly.

5. End on the actual business promise.
   - Consider replacing `The unmissable clinic.` with a line closer to `Recover missed patients before they leak.`

## Do Not Do

- do not redesign the whole film
- do not add more than one extra scene
- do not turn it into a generic AI product reel
- do not spend morning time on animation perfection

## If One Small Feature Is Added

Add a tiny ROI caption in the final third:

`Missed call recovered. Patient qualified. Booking saved.`

That is the cleanest extra beat if you want a conversion lift without a rebuild.
