## Design Rules

- Keep it to one page if possible (two max)
- Use clean tables, not paragraphs
- Bold the key numbers
- Always include the "One Action" recommendation
- Always include the disclaimer on revenue estimates
- Make it look premium — this is the retention anchor

## Delivery

- Send every [agreed day] via WhatsApp or email
- Include a short WhatsApp message:

```
Here is this week's Jawab Intelligence Report for [Clinic].

- [X] patient opportunities detected
- [Y] recovered
- [Z] qualified and routed to staff

One recommendation: [short version of the recommendation]

Full report attached.
```
