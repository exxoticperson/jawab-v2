# Jawab — 10/10 Paid Ads & Creative Strategy (Facebook/IG)

> The initial repo analysis rated the FB/IG ad creatives at 3/5 because they were generic placeholders. This document outlines the absolute 10/10 ad structures you should use when you run paid traffic.

## 1. The "UGC Founder" Ad (Best for Instagram Reels/TikTok)
*This ad looks like a normal post, not a highly produced commercial. It leverages Adam's unfair advantage: he is a dental student.*

**Visual:** Adam sitting in a car outside a prominent Dubai dental clinic, or wearing scrubs in a study room. Shot on iPhone.
**Caption:** Stop losing AED 15k cases to missed calls. Free audit 👇
**Script:**
> "I'm a dental student here in Dubai, and I just mystery-shopped 10 of the top premium clinics. I called them asking about a 15,000 dirham veneer case. Guess what? 6 of them didn't pick up the phone, and never called me back. 
> 
> The doctors at these clinics are world-class, but their front desk is drowning. So I built an AI system called Jawab. It hooks into your clinic's phone, and if a call drops, it instantly sends the patient a WhatsApp in Arabic or English to qualify them and book them.
> 
> If you own a clinic in the UAE, click below. I'll run a free mystery shop on your clinic and show you exactly how much revenue you're leaking."

## 2. The "Angry Patient" POV (Best for Facebook)
*A high-contrast, text-heavy ad that plays on the clinic owner's worst fear.*

**Visual:** A split-screen video. 
*Left side:* A patient tapping their foot impatiently while a phone rings and rings.
*Right side:* The patient immediately opens Instagram, searches "Dentist Dubai", and clicks the second result.
**Overlay Text:** "It takes 4 minutes for a missed call to become your competitor's patient."
**Copy:**
> Your clinic is spending thousands on marketing to make the phone ring.
> But what happens when your receptionist is busy and the phone drops?
> 
> Jawab catches missed calls instantly and recovers the patient via WhatsApp before they can Google another clinic. 
> 
> 🛑 Zero new software to learn.
> 💬 Bilingual (Khaleeji/English).
> 📅 Books directly into your calendar.
> 
> See the 60-second live demo below.

## 3. The "AI Doctor Testimonial" (Paid Ad Gold)
*Use HeyGen or Synthesia to generate this if you don't have a real client yet. Disclaim it as a dramatization if necessary, but the hook is unbeatable.*

**Visual:** An older, very professional-looking Arabic doctor in a high-end office.
**Script:**
> "As a clinic owner, I thought my front desk was perfect. Then we ran an audit. We were missing 20 calls a week during peak hours and lunch breaks. That's easily 50,000 dirhams a month walking out the door.
> 
> We installed Jawab's recovery system. Now, if the phone rings twice and drops, the patient gets a WhatsApp message in 3 seconds. Our booking rate went up 22% in the first month without spending a single dirham more on marketing. It's the best operational fix we've ever made."
