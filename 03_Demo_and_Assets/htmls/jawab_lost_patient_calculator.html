Math logic
Potential at-risk patient value

Missed calls and slow replies are converted into a monthly opportunity pool, then combined with post-consult drop-offs.

MonthlyLeak = ((MissedCalls + SlowReplies) * 4 + Dropoffs) * TreatmentValue
Conservative recovery opportunity

Jawab does not need to recover everything. It only needs to rescue a conservative slice of the existing leak.

RecoverableValue = MonthlyLeak * RecoveryRate
Potential coverage vs Jawab cost

The ratio compares conservative recovery opportunity to the current Jawab monthly retainer after the first month.

SimpleROI = RecoverableValue / 1500

This calculator is intentionally conservative and should be presented as a discussion tool. It supports the Jawab audit and discovery process; it is not a claim of guaranteed clinic revenue.
