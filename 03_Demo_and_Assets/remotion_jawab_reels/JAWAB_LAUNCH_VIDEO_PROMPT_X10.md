# Jawab Launch Film Prompt x10

## Objective

Create one premium cinematic launch video for Jawab, using `2.png` as a composition reference, not a flat background.

The final video should make Jawab feel like the future operating standard for premium Gulf clinics: dental, aesthetic, dermatology, cosmetic surgery, and high-end medical brands.

## Reference Image Layer Map

Each visual element from the reference is separated into its own animated layer:

- Black graphite stage: procedural dark clinic-tech environment with architectural panel lines.
- Marble shard, top-left: floating luxury material cue, slow parallax.
- Marble block, bottom-right: physical premium anchor for the final tableau.
- Central phone: independent product object with rotating inbox, handoff, and report states.
- Response Time card: floating metric layer, detached and animated.
- Conversations card: floating volume/proof layer, detached and animated.
- Booked Appointments card: booking proof layer, detached and animated.
- Quote card: premium belief statement, detached and animated.
- Analytics panel: weekly intelligence/report moment.
- Gold loop mark: brand signal and "never miss" metaphor.
- Gold pen: luxury desk object, subtle lower-frame motion.
- Gold micro-lines: signal flow and revenue recovery paths.

## Product Story

Jawab is not an AI receptionist.

Jawab is a Clinic Revenue Leak Audit + Patient Recovery System.

It finds and fixes:

- missed calls
- slow WhatsApp replies
- unread DMs
- after-hours inquiries
- post-consult drop-offs
- weak staff handoff
- missing owner visibility

## Narrative Arc

0-9s: Brand world.

Jawab appears as a luxury clinic intelligence system. The viewer should feel: this already operates at a high level.

9-17s: The leak.

Premium clinics lose patients before the first appointment. The enemy is not bad dentistry, it is silence.

17-26s: What Jawab is.

A revenue recovery layer for premium clinics. Not a chatbot. Not staff replacement.

26-38s: How it works.

Detect, recover, qualify, handoff. Arabic and English. WhatsApp-first. Staff receives clear next action.

38-49s: Owner visibility.

Weekly intelligence report: detected inquiries, recovered conversations, qualified leads, booked consults, bottlenecks.

49-60s: Launch close.

The future of clinic communication starts before reception answers.

## On-Screen Text System

- Use short, high-status statements.
- Place critical text in the upper-middle Instagram safe area.
- Avoid bottom-heavy captions except final CTA pill.
- Use white for authority, champagne gold for key emotional words, muted grey for explanation.

## Motion Direction

- The reference image starts as a scattered product tableau.
- Layers lift independently: phone rises, cards drift, marble rotates, gold lines draw.
- Scenes should feel like a luxury product keynote colliding with a clinical intelligence film.
- Maintain motion every 0.5-2 seconds through parallax, card reveals, line draws, metric shifts, and impact flashes.

## Sound Direction

- Low-frequency tension bed.
- Sparse tick sounds for UI and metric changes.
- Bass impacts on scene turns.
- Controlled, expensive, quiet pressure.
